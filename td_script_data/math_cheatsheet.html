💡 Quick Tips for td_math2img:
  • Outer delimiters like $...$ or $$...$$ are optional; td_math2img handles them automatically.
  • Use --save formula.png or --save formula.svg to save to a specific file path.
  • Use -s or --show to open the rendered image directly in swayimg.
  • Use -t for transparent background, or -d for dark theme.
